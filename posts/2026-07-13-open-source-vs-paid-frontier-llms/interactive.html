The price of intelligence

Every frontier model, plotted by how much a unit of intelligence actually costs against how capable it is. The horizontal axis is the real bill — the cost of one Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index task, reasoning tokens included. Open weights own the cheap end of the curve, and Kimi K3 holds the rung open models opened at the top. What changed since July: Claude Opus 5 took the lead outright, OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna's price by 80%, and DeepSeek's re-post-trained V4 Flash landed at index 50 for three cents a task. The dashed line is the efficient frontier — the most intelligence available at each cost-per-task. Seven models hold it: DeepSeek V4 Flash, GPT-5.6 Luna, Grok 4.5, GPT-5.6 Terra, Kimi K3, GPT-5.6 Sol, and Claude Opus 5 at the ceiling. Two of those seven are open weights, and they hold the two ends that matter.

DeepSeek's re-post-trained V4 Flash is the story of this update: index 50 at roughly $0.03 a task — the same score as Gemini 3.5 Flash and Gemini 3.6 Flash for about a fourteenth of their cost. It anchors the entire cheap half of the curve, and nothing paid comes close to it down there.

At the top, Kimi K3 still holds the rung open weights opened in July — index 57, fourth in the world — but at about $1.11 a task it is no longer cheap. The paid tiers did move: OpenAI's 80% cut puts GPT-5.6 Luna on the frontier at index 51, the best value of anything closed. On the value axis the gap is still not close — DeepSeek V4 Flash returns around 1,500 index points per task-dollar against Luna's ~645 and Claude Fable 5's ~23.
